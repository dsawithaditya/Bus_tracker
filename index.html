How It Works

Practical features built specifically for the day-to-day realities of campus transit.

📍

Smooth Gliding GPS

Buses update position every 3 seconds and interpolate smoothly along the road line rather than jumping across the map.

⏱️

Traffic-Adjusted ETAs

Arrival times calculate distance, recent segment speed, traffic bottlenecks, and stop dwell times with confidence ranges.

🚶

Walk vs. Bus Timing

Compares your walking distance to the stop against the bus's live arrival time, showing whether you can walk normally or need to hurry.

🚨

Standby Bus Dispatch

When an unexpected delay or mechanical breakdown occurs, dispatchers can reroute the nearest standby bus in one click.

💬 Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

ETA & GPS How are stop arrival times calculated?
↓
Arrival times use remaining route distance divided by rolling speed on that road segment, scaled by real-time traffic factors and stop dwell times.
NETWORK What happens if cellular reception drops?
↓
If no GPS ping is received for 15s, the bus marker is labeled 'Estimated position' and glides along the route at its last speed. After 2 minutes, it falls back to timetable.
PRIVACY Is student or commuter location tracked?
↓
No. All walking distance calculations and proximity alarms run entirely locally inside your browser. No personal location data is ever stored.
CROWD How does seat occupancy reporting work?
↓
Commuters can submit crowd levels. To guarantee accuracy, verified driver and conductor reports immediately take precedence.
