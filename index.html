Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Smart Arrival Prediction (ETA) calculate times? ↓
The engine computes remaining route distance divided by the bus's rolling 5-minute speed, multiplied by a real-time traffic factor, plus the scheduled dwell times of all intermediate stops. It also provides confidence ranges (e.g. 11 min [9 to 13]) that widen under abnormal traffic conditions.
What happens in dead zones or when cellular signal drops? ↓
If no GPS ping is received for 15 seconds, the bus marker is labeled "Estimated position" and continues moving at its last recorded speed along the road line. If no signal returns after 2 minutes, a "Signal lost" badge is displayed and the app falls back to timetable estimates.
Is student location stored or tracked? ↓
No. In accordance with Section 7.3 and 12.2 of the project blueprint, student walking distances and geofences are computed entirely locally on the student's phone. No student location data is sent to or stored on backend servers.
How does the Conductor override work on crowd occupancy? ↓
Students can submit crowd reports (Seats, Standing, Full), which use a majority rule from the last 3 reports within 10 minutes. However, a verified report from the driver or conductor immediately overrides student reports to prevent false alerts.
