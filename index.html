How It Works

Practical features built specifically for the day-to-day realities of campus transit.

📍

Smooth Gliding GPS

Buses update position every 3 seconds and interpolate smoothly along the road line rather than jumping across the map.

⏱️

Traffic-Adjusted ETAs

Arrival times calculate distance, recent segment speed, traffic bottlenecks, and stop dwell times with confidence ranges.

🚶

Walk vs. Bus Timing

Compares your walking distance to the stop against the bus's live arrival time, showing whether you can walk normally or need to hurry.

🚨

Standby Bus Dispatch

When an unexpected delay or mechanical breakdown occurs, dispatchers can reroute the nearest standby bus in one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are stop arrival times calculated? ↓
Arrival times use remaining route distance divided by recent rolling speed on that road segment, scaled by a real-time traffic multiplier, plus scheduled passenger dwell time at intermediate stops.
What happens if cellular reception drops? ↓
If no GPS ping is received for 15 seconds, the bus marker is labeled "Estimated position" and continues along the planned route at its last known speed. After 2 minutes without reception, it displays a "Signal lost" badge and defaults to the timetable.
Is student location tracked or stored? ↓
No. All walking distance calculations and proximity alarms run locally in the student's browser. No rider location data is ever transmitted to or stored on servers.
How does occupancy reporting work? ↓
Riders can submit seat availability status, which takes the majority vote of the last 3 reports within 10 minutes. A driver or conductor update immediately overrides student reports to prevent inaccurate crowd estimates.
